#### Rules and Regulations
- [On Premises Alcohol](https://www.worcesterma.gov/inspections/document-center/on-premises-rules.pdf)
	- **RULE #6** Records of all alcoholic beverages sold and purchased must be kept and made available when called for by the Commissioner or their agents. 
	- **RULE #25** Separate toilet and lavatory facilities for men and women required on premises
	- **RULE #28** Managers and all persons involved in the service of liquor must complete a server training program approved by the License Commission prior to serving any alcoholic beverages and in all cases, within thirty (30) calendar days of their first day of work. Persons serving alcohol under a one-day special license must be trained prior to service. The manager's training certificate must be filed with the License Commission prior to the commencement of alcohol service at the premises. Copies of each employee or volunteer’s server training certificate must be kept on the licensed premises and made available upon request.
